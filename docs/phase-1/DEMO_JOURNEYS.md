# Demo and Acceptance Journeys

## Journey A - Social order to accountable cash

**Business:** Cedar & Thread, a Beirut fashion seller using Instagram and WhatsApp.

1. An employee creates an Instagram order for two variants in USD with a delivery fee and Whish prepayment.
2. The customer opens the WhatsApp confirmation link and supplies an Achrafieh address, building/floor, landmark, and pin.
3. Employees prepare and pack the order; dispatcher assigns an internal driver.
4. Driver goes offline, marks Delivered, records cash collected for the remaining balance, and reconnects.
5. The action synchronizes once; delivery is Delivered and payment is Paid through two entries.
6. Cash Map shows the cash remainder held by the driver until handover.
7. Driver returns less cash than expected; reconciliation opens a shortage with explanation and owner review.

**Proof:** no manual chat copying, no duplicate offline command, delivery/payment remain separate, and cash custody is traceable.

## Journey B - Supplier shock and restock decision

**Business:** same tenant; a supplier raises a product cost in USD and lead time increases.

1. Owner records the new supplier cost and lead time without changing historical orders.
2. Currency & Margin Guard previews the affected selling-price and contribution range.
3. Stock & Supplier Radar finds a best seller with seven days of cover and a slow product holding cash.
4. Masaar proposes an explainable restock quantity within a USD budget and highlights dead stock that could be discounted.
5. Owner edits the quantity, approves selected price changes, and rejects automatic changes to other products.

**Proof:** the recommendation exposes formula/inputs, currency is explicit, and approval remains human.

## Journey C - Sales rose but contribution fell

1. Morning Brief detects higher revenue but lower contribution versus the previous comparable period.
2. Profit Leak Explorer attributes the change to supplier cost, a broad discount, and failed delivery expense in one area.
3. Lebanon Delivery Map drills into the area's attempts, failed reasons, delivery fee, and contribution.
4. Channel & Customer Pulse shows that WhatsApp repeat buyers remain profitable while a promoted Instagram product/channel combination is not.
5. Scenario Mode previews removing the discount and adjusting the area fee.

**Proof:** each explanation reconciles to source orders/costs/attempts and proposes reversible actions rather than claiming AI certainty.

## Journey D - Honest incomplete data

1. An order lacks a product cost snapshot.
2. Masaar reports revenue and collections but labels product/channel contribution incomplete.
3. Morning Brief requests the missing cost instead of presenting false profit.

**Proof:** missing data degrades confidence visibly and never becomes zero cost.
