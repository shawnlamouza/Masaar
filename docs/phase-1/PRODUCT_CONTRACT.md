# Masaar Product Contract

## One-sentence promise

Masaar turns scattered Lebanese social-commerce operations into one accountable flow and tells the owner what changed, why it matters, and what action to take.

## Primary customer

The MVP is designed for an owner-managed Lebanese product business with approximately 50-1,000 monthly orders, a small team, social or phone-based sales, delivery fulfillment, mixed payment methods, and limited operational software.

Initial pilot categories should favor variant/stock-sensitive products such as fashion, accessories, beauty, gifts, handmade goods, specialty food, and general retail. Service-only businesses are not the first design center because inventory, supplier cost, and delivery accountability are central differentiators.

## Real problem

Orders and customer details are copied between chats, screenshots, paper, spreadsheets, and memory. Delivery can be completed while payment remains unclear; cash can remain with a driver; USD/LBP or supplier-cost changes can erase margin; low stock is noticed too late; dead stock consumes scarce cash; and owners cannot explain why profit, cash, or customer activity changed.

## Product boundary

Masaar begins when a customer intends to order and ends when the resulting operational and financial decision is understood. The MVP includes:

- catalog, variants, prices, costs, suppliers, customers, and Lebanese addresses;
- Quick Order, secure customer confirmation, lifecycle processing, and audit history;
- driver assignment, attempts, zones, fees, and offline status capture;
- separate delivery and payment states for Cash, Whish, OMT, Card, Bank, and Other;
- cash custody, handover, reconciliation, discrepancies, and daily close;
- stock movements, reservations, low/dead-stock alerts, returns, and restock guidance;
- owner decision intelligence with evidence and reversible recommended actions.

## Explicit non-goals

- Replacing Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, or Facebook.
- Scraping private messages or using unofficial messaging automation.
- Full accounting, tax filing, payroll, NSSF filing, banking, or legal advice.
- A broad public marketplace or full enterprise ERP.
- Automatic purchasing, pricing, refunds, customer rejection, or other irreversible AI action.
- Cross-business customer blacklists.

## Hero product loop

1. An employee records or receives an order request.
2. The customer confirms contact and Lebanese address details through a secure link.
3. The team prepares the order and assigns delivery.
4. The driver records attempts and collections, even during temporary connectivity loss.
5. Masaar records payment independently, tracks custody, and reconciles returned money.
6. Inventory and product economics update from explicit events.
7. The owner receives a ranked Morning Brief with cause, estimated impact, evidence, and action.

## Hero decision experiences

- **Morning Brief:** five ranked issues or opportunities.
- **Profit Leak Explorer:** explains the gap between expected margin and realized contribution.
- **Cash Map:** shows payment status, currency, current holder, age, and discrepancy.
- **Price & Stock Radar:** connects supplier cost, FX reference, margin, stock cover, and restock cash.
- **Lebanon Delivery Map:** compares area success, failed reasons, fees, delay, and contribution.
- **Channel & Customer Pulse:** compares channel/product value and identifies lapsed regular customers.
- **Scenario Mode:** previews owner-entered cost, FX, price, discount, and delivery-fee changes.

## MVP success measures

The pilot is successful when representative users can:

- capture a normal existing-customer order in 60 seconds or less;
- find the owner of any pending operational action in less than 15 seconds;
- trace any collected amount to a method, currency, holder, and source order;
- reconcile a driver's expected and returned cash without a spreadsheet;
- explain a margin or stock warning from its source records;
- identify at least one useful action from the Morning Brief without analytics training;
- complete the golden path under simulated slow or interrupted connectivity.

## Scope-change rule

A new MVP feature must replace an existing item, not simply join the scope. It must solve a validated problem, reuse Masaar's operational data, fit the golden path, and have a measurable acceptance criterion.
