# Masaar 1:45 competition demo

## Before recording

- Run `pnpm dev`, open `http://localhost:5173`, and select **Joe · Owner**.
- Use the seeded demonstration records; keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Record at 1080p. Do not claim email, WhatsApp, courier, payment-provider or public AWS integrations are live unless their credentials and release gates are complete.

## 0:00–0:15 — Owner problem and first action

“Lebanese social sellers close sales in messages, then manually copy orders, addresses, delivery updates and payments across chats and spreadsheets. Masaar becomes the operational system after the customer decides to order—and turns that accountable workflow into decisions the owner can act on.”

Show the owner Overview and the Action Center. Point to orders in progress, delivery success, cash held outside the business and the next actions.

## 0:15–0:45 — Capture one reliable order

Open **Orders → Quick order**. Show the source channel, existing/new customer choice, exact product variant/SKU, delivery zone, payment expectation and server-calculated total. Explain that the secure confirmation link lets the customer provide a standardized Lebanese address without an account, eliminating repeated copying from WhatsApp.

## 0:45–1:15 — Delivery, quick driver cut and cash accountability

Open **Delivery**. Show configurable internal drivers, freelancers and companies, Lebanese zones, multiple attempts and a failed-delivery reason. Cut to Karim’s phone view for only 5–10 seconds: address, amount, attempt and collection.

Open **Payments**. Say: “Delivered is not the same as paid. When a driver records a collection, Masaar posts the payment and assigns cash custody automatically. The money remains with that holder until an owner or manager accepts the handover; discrepancies require an explanation.”

## 1:15–1:40 — Business intelligence that changes a decision

Open **Intelligence**. Show the Morning Brief, revenue/profit/collected separation, Profit and Cash truth, product/channel economics and Lebanon area growth map. Explain that LBP records use an owner-approved exchange-rate snapshot, incomplete costs are disclosed, and every warning links back to operational records.

Use one example: collected cash outside the register, low stock/restock budget, or an area where incorrect addresses drive failures.

## 1:40–1:45 — Close

“Masaar is intentionally not another generic ERP. It owns one difficult loop: capture the operation, protect the money and stock, explain the impact, and tell a Lebanese owner what to do next—with evidence.”

End on the Overview or Intelligence hero. Add the deployed URL and GitHub URL to the submission PDF before sending it.
